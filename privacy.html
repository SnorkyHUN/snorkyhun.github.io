Clipforge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Clipforge is a desktop application for Windows that turns your own videos into short clips. This policy explains what data Clipforge handles and how.

1. Where your data is stored

Clipforge runs on your own computer. Your videos, clips, transcripts and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that collects or stores your videos or personal data.

2. Connected accounts (YouTube, TikTok)

You can choose to connect your YouTube or TikTok account. When you do:

  • You sign in on the official Google or TikTok website. Clipforge never sees your password.
  • Clipforge receives access tokens, which are stored on your computer in encrypted form (using the operating system's secure storage).
  • From TikTok, Clipforge reads only your basic profile information (display name and avatar) to show which account is connected, and uses the video upload / publish permissions only to send a video when you click the upload button.
  • Clipforge uploads or posts only after you start it: by pressing an upload button, or by starting the optional AUTO AI mode, which then uploads clips on its own until you press Stop.

You can disconnect an account at any time in Clipforge's Settings. This deletes the stored tokens and revokes the access. You can also remove access in your TikTok or Google account settings.

3. Data we share

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data. Videos and their titles/descriptions are sent only to the platform you choose (YouTube or TikTok) when you upload them. If you enable optional cloud AI features with your own API key, the related text or audio is sent to that AI provider to process your request.

4. Analytics and advertising

Clipforge does not contain advertising and does not use tracking or analytics services.

5. Children

Clipforge is not intended for children under 13.

6. Deleting your data

Because your data is stored on your computer, you can delete it at any time by deleting your projects in Clipforge or uninstalling the application. Disconnecting an account removes its tokens.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
